# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 14, 2026_

SheetPilot ("the add-on") is a Google Sheets™ extension built with Google Apps Script.

## What data SheetPilot accesses

SheetPilot only reads and modifies the content of the Google Sheets™ spreadsheet you have open and active while using the add-on (cell values, and cell background color for the "Highlight Issues" feature). SheetPilot does not access any other spreadsheet, file, or Google service on your account.

## What SheetPilot does NOT do

- SheetPilot does not send, transmit, or store your spreadsheet data anywhere outside of Google's own infrastructure. All analysis runs inside the Apps Script environment tied to your Google Account.
- SheetPilot does not use any external servers, APIs, or third-party services.
- SheetPilot does not include analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
- SheetPilot does not share, sell, or disclose your data to any third party, because it never leaves your Google Account in the first place.

## Data retention

SheetPilot does not persist any data of its own. Nothing is stored beyond the current session — closing the sidebar or the spreadsheet ends SheetPilot's access to that data.

## Permissions requested

SheetPilot requests only the minimum Google Sheets™ permission needed to read and edit the currently active spreadsheet. It does not request access to Google Drive™, Gmail™, Calendar™, or any other Google service.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as SheetPilot's feature set grows (for example, if a future version adds capabilities that require different permissions). Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.
